# Imagery — image concept & image suggestions

> Template. Project copy lives at `.claude/design-manager/design-system/imagery.md`; fill it for your project.

> The `ux-designer` understands the **visual concept** of the project (and of its reference site) before suggesting images. For empty `media` fields it makes **short, concrete image suggestions** instead of only flagging them.

## Mandatory behaviour

- **Look at and understand the references:** before suggesting images, study the image concept of the project's **own** site and of a **reference** site — motif world, style, color, role of images. If tools are available, look at the real images/alt texts.
- **Empty `media` field:** a 1-sentence image suggestion (What does the image show? Style?) matching the concept and the section's purpose. Not just "_TBD_".
- **`fit` vs `fill`:** background/hero media default to **`fill`/cover** (filling the format), not `fit` — set the option correctly even when the URL is still missing. Avoid a distorted/letterbox look.
- **Alt text** is mandatory (AA) — think of it together with the suggestion.
